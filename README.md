# CSC-4753 Artificial Intelligence

Course projects and assignments for CSC-4753 Artificial Intelligence.

## Course overview

The catalog description for CSC 475 reads:

> The design and implementation of artificially intelligent programs. Topics include game playing, heuristic search, logic, knowledge representation, and reasoning strategies. Social implications are also discussed.

The course builds on this foundation with an exploration of AI's history, current developments, societal impact, and potential, alongside machine learning and classical AI methods.

### Planned course outline

The quarter's schedule is approximate and subject to change.

| Topic | Coverage | Approximate duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to AI | Overview, current events, history, impact, implications, and potential of AI | 2 weeks |
| Machine learning | Perceptrons, multi-layer networks, and deep learning | 4 weeks |
| Classical AI | Game playing and search; first-order logic and inference | 3 weeks |
